Ideogram 4.0 also does very well with inpainting. It makes it easy to generate at lower megapixels and then mask 
and inpaint areas like faces to clean up and correct detail.
I use the Comfyui-Inpaint-CropAndStitch custom node , personally, but most of the time Ideogram 4.0 doesn't need it.
